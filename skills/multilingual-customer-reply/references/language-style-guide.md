# Language Style Guide

## General

- Reply in the customer's language unless the message is clearly multilingual and an English business reply is the established channel language.
- Keep trade terms and Incoterms (FOB, CIF, DDP, EXW) untranslated but explain briefly only if needed.
- Preserve digits, dates, currency, SKU, order, PI, and tracking numbers exactly.

## English

- B2B tone: direct, courteous, no over-familiarity.
- Use `Dear [Name]` in email, clear subject lines, and a single clear next action.

## Spanish

- Default to `usted` for unknown contacts and formal business; use `tu` only when the customer does first.
- Distinguish Latin American and Castilian phrasing; prefer neutral vocabulary when uncertain.

## Arabic

- Use Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) for formal business unless the customer writes in a clear dialect and expects matching tone.
- Keep numbers, SKUs, and prices in Western digits when they are business data.
- Ensure the message respects right-to-left layout; keep Latin identifiers intact.

## French

- Default to `vous`; switch to `tu` only if the customer does first.
- Preserve accents and formal business closings.

## Portuguese

- Distinguish Brazilian and European Portuguese; use `você`/`o senhor, a senhora` forms for Brazil and `o senhor, a senhora` or `você` per context.
- Keep trade vocabulary consistent with the customer's region.

## Russian

- Use `вы` for formal business unless the customer uses `ты`.
- Keep dates unambiguous; prefer ISO-like or explicit formats for deadlines.

## German

- Use `Sie` for formal business unless the customer uses `du`.
- Keep grammar formal and precise; avoid marketing exaggeration.

## Chinese

- Use `您` for formal B2B unless the customer uses `你`.
- Keep amounts, dates, and product codes exact; avoid vague time words for commitments.

## When to ask for clarification

- The detected language is uncertain or the message mixes languages.
- The request is too ambiguous to answer safely even after translation.
- The content has legal/compliance weight; confirm with a human before sending.
