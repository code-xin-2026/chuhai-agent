# Trade Reply Templates

Translate and adapt each message to the customer's language. Replace `[placeholders]` with verified data or remove the sentence.

## Quotation follow-up

Subject: Quotation for [Product / SKU]

Dear [Customer name],

Please find our quotation for [quantity] [product] under [Incoterm] to [destination]. The quotation is valid until [date] and is based on [key specification or assumption].

The next step is [sample approval / payment / specification confirmation]. Please confirm [decision needed] so we can proceed.

Best regards,
[Name]

## Quotation detail request

Dear [Customer name],

Thank you for your interest in [product]. To prepare an accurate quotation, please confirm:

- Required model or specification
- Quantity
- Destination country and port
- Required delivery date
- Required Incoterm
- Any mandatory certification or labeling

We will review the details and send the next update by [follow-up time].

## Delay holding response

Dear [Customer name],

We understand that delivery timing is critical for [customer impact]. We are checking shipment [reference] with [responsible team] because [verified issue].

We do not yet have a confirmed revised delivery date. We will provide an update by [follow-up time] with the verified ETA and available recovery options.

## Quality complaint acknowledgement

Dear [Customer name],

We are sorry to hear about [specific issue] with [product / order]. Thank you for providing [evidence received].

We have opened case [case reference] and [owner/team] is reviewing the affected quantity, batch, and delivery details. We will contact you by [follow-up time] with the findings and next steps.
