# Trade Reply Templates

Use this skill to draft consistent replies for recurring foreign-trade situations. Treat templates as a starting point; adapt to the customer, language, and verified facts.

## Rules

1. Replace every placeholder with verified information or remove the sentence. Never send unresolved placeholders.
2. Lead with the customer's actual question or the impact, not a long introduction.
3. Use a professional B2B tone: direct, calm, specific, accountable.
4. Do not admit liability or promise compensation, refunds, or dates without approved data.
5. If the situation is risky, route it through `trade-risk-escalation` before sending.

## Situations covered

- Quotation follow-up and quotation detail request.
- Proforma invoice (PI) confirmation.
- Delivery or logistics delay.
- Quality complaint acknowledgement.
- Refund, replacement, or claim response.
- Sample request and dispatch update.
- Payment confirmation and documentation request.

Read `references/templates.md` for the full message templates and the escalation checks that accompany each one.
